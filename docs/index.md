```sh
composer require omnimap/maplibre
```

## What it answers

The contracts of omniscient/omnimap this provider implements, and what it does not (each refused with the
family's "not supported" exception).

## Errors

The family's rule (see omniscient/omnimap's docs): the service's refusals and unreadable answers are its
provider exception, a request that got no answer its unreachable one.
